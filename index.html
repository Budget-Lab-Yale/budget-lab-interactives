Interactive tools

Interactive web tools published by The Budget Lab at Yale. Each tool can be embedded on the Budget Lab website and other host pages.

Tools

  • Deficits and Affordability
    How federal deficits raise borrowing costs on auto loans, mortgages, and small business loans — with a calculator for your own loan amount.
  • Deficit Impact Calculator
    Calculator-only variant of the Deficits and Affordability tool. Estimate the impact of federal deficits on borrowing costs for a specific loan.
