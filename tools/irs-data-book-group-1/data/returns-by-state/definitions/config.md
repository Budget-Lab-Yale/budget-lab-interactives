## Where a return is counted

- By the filer's address. “Sole proprietors, partners in a partnership, or shareholders in an S corporation may use their business addresses.” (Table 1-7, FY2022 book)
- **FY1998-99** are reported by IRS district. A district that is one whole state fills that state; the others are grey.
- **Puerto Rico and returns from abroad** are in the U.S. total and the full table, not on the map.

## Types of return

- **Supplemental documents**: from FY2022 they include the electronic Form 1040-X (shared/config/definition_breaks.csv).
- **Per 1,000 residents**: our figure, with the Census Bureau's July 1 population estimate (FRED series <state>POP).
