## Measures

- **Gross collections**: taxes collected. “Gross collections include penalties and interest in addition to taxes.” (FY2024 book, note 1)
- **Refunds**: “Includes overpayment refunds, refunds resulting from examination activity, refundable tax credits, and other outlays required by law.” They include interest paid on refunds, and exclude “refunds credited to taxpayer accounts for tax liability in a subsequent year” (FY2025 book, note 2). Stimulus and pandemic payments count as refunds (FY2008 book, note 4; FY2020-2022 books, note 5).
- **Net collections**: gross collections less refunds. The IRS makes no refund estimate for the parts of income and OASDHI taxes, so net collections exist only for the five types of tax and the total (FY2024 book, note 4).
- **Share of total**: the IRS's own printed percentage of U.S. gross or net collections. For refunds, which the IRS prints no share of, it is our figure: each type's refunds over total refunds.

## Types of tax

- **Individual income taxes**: income tax withheld, and payments, which “Includes collections of estimated income tax and payments made in conjunction with individual income tax return filings.” (FY2024 book, note 5). Also estate and trust income tax. The FY2008 book on prints payments and estate and trust income tax separately; earlier books print them as one line, `Other`.
- **Employment taxes**: “Includes taxes for Old-Age, Survivors, Disability, and Hospital Insurance (OASDHI); unemployment insurance under the Federal Unemployment Tax Act (FUTA); and railroad retirement under the Railroad Retirement Tax Act (RRTA).” (Table 1-6, FY2025 book, note 4). OASDHI is FICA, on wages, and SECA, on self-employment income. “The OASDHI tax collections and refunds shown in this table are based on estimates made by the Secretary of the Treasury” (FY2024 book, note 4).
- **Business income taxes**: “Includes taxes on corporation income (Form 1120 series) and on unrelated business income from tax-exempt organizations (Form 990-T).” (Table 1-6, FY2025 book, note 2). Partnerships, S corporations, regulated investment companies, and real estate investment trusts are not shown: “these entities generally do not have a tax liability” (FY2024 book).
- **Excise taxes**: “Excludes excise taxes collected by U.S. Customs and Border Protection and the Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau.” (FY2024 book, note 7)
- **Estate and gift taxes**: the federal estate tax and gift tax.

## Years and dollars

- **Fiscal year**: October through September. “FY 1977 covered October 1976 through September 1977” (Table 1-6, FY2025 book, note 6).
- **Nominal dollars**: as printed. “All money amounts are in current dollars.” (FY2024 book)
- **FY2025 dollars**: our conversion with the GDP price index (FRED GDPDEF, retrieved September 29, 2026), averaged over each fiscal year.
- “Collection and refund data may not be comparable for a given fiscal year because payments made in prior years may be refunded in the current fiscal year.” (FY2024 book)

## Known problems in the source

- **FY2015 excise refunds** are printed as $7,445,780 thousand. The same row's net collections, $76,457,302 thousand, is gross collections of $77,201,882 thousand less $744,580 thousand: the printed figure has an extra digit. The charts draw it as printed.
- **FY1999 OASDHI net collections**, $585,974,264 thousand, are not gross less refunds ($583,607,786 thousand).
