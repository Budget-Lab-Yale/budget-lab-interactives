## Types of return

- **Individual income tax returns**: “Includes the Form 1040 series as follows: 1040; 1040-NR (nonresident alien income tax return)”…” (FY2025 book, note 3). Forms 1040A and 1040EZ are printed apart through the FY2000 book.
- **S corporations**: “are not strictly income tax returns because S corporations are not taxed directly” (FY2005 book, note 5), but the IRS counts them with income tax returns.
- **Supplemental documents**: amended returns and extensions. “Includes Forms 1040X (amended individual income tax)”…” (FY1999 book, note 6). From FY2022 they include the electronic Form 1040-X.
- **Not counted**: the table “Excludes information returns” (FY2008 book, note 1), such as Forms 1099 and W-2.

## Figures

- **Change from prior year**: the IRS's own figure. “Percentage changes are based on rounded data.” (FY2020 book)
- **Counts**: some books print a few rows in thousands of returns. Every figure here is a count.
