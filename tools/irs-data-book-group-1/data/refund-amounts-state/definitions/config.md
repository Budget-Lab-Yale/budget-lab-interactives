## What a refund is

- “Includes overpayment refunds, refunds resulting from examination activity, refundable tax credits, and other outlays required by law.” “Also includes $16.8 billion in interest”… (FY2025 book, note 1)
- **Economic impact payments**: the pandemic laws “included provisions to distribute economic impact payments to qualified taxpayers” (Table 1-7, FY2022 book, note 3).
- **Advance child tax credit**: “The ARP Act included provisions to distribute monthly advance child tax credit payments to qualified taxpayers from July 2021 through December 2021.” (Table 1-7, FY2022 book, note 4)
- **Stimulus payments, FY2008-09**: the Economic Stimulus Act of 2008 payments, printed in their own column.

## Where a refund is counted

- By the filer's address. “Sole proprietors, partners in a partnership, or shareholders in an S corporation may use their business addresses.” (Table 1-7, FY2022 book)
- **FY1995-97** are reported by IRS district. A state has a figure where its district was the whole state, or where the book printed a memo total for the state; the rest are grey.

## Our figures

- **Less stimulus and advance payments**: the printed figure less the IRS's own payment columns.
- **Average refund**: this table over Table 1-7, same state, type, and year.
- **Per resident**: with the Census Bureau's July 1 population estimate (FRED series <state>POP).
