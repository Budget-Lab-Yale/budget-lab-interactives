## Measures

- **Gross collections**: “Gross collections include penalties and interest in addition to taxes.” (FY2025 book, note 1)
- **From FY2009**: “Beginning with Fiscal Year 2009, adjustments and credits are excluded.” (FY2009 book, note 1)
- **Individual income taxes**: “Beginning with FY 2008, estate and trust income tax is reported separately.” (FY2025 book, note 3). The charts add the two back together in every year.
- **Employment taxes**: “Includes taxes for Old-Age, Survivors, Disability, and Hospital Insurance (OASDHI); unemployment insurance under the Federal Unemployment Tax Act (FUTA); and railroad retirement under the Railroad Retirement Tax Act (RRTA).” (FY2025 book, note 4)
- **Business income taxes**: “Includes taxes on corporation income (Form 1120 series) and on unrelated business income from tax-exempt organizations (Form 990-T).” (FY2025 book, note 2)

## Years and dollars

- **Fiscal year**: “FY 1976 covered July 1975 through June 1976”, and “FY 1977 covered October 1976 through September 1977” (FY2025 book, note 6). The quarter between them is not in these figures.
- **FY2025 dollars**: our conversion with the GDP price index (FRED GDPDEF, retrieved September 29, 2026), averaged over each fiscal year: July-June through FY1976, October-September from FY1977.
