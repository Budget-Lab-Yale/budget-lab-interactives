## What a state's figure is

- “Receipts shown for the various States do not indicate the Federal tax burden of each, since, in many instances, taxes are collected in one State from residents of, or operations in, another.” (FY1999 book, note 1)
- “Classification by State is usually based on taxpayer”’s address, or for businesses the principal office (FY2000 book, note 1).
- **From FY2005**: “Adjustments and credits are not shown by State, but are included in the U.S. totals.” (FY2005 book, note 5)

## Cells that are not the state's own

- **Maryland and DC, FY1998-2001**: one line for both; both tiles are grey.
- **California and Arkansas, FY2006 estate and gift tax**: combined by the IRS to avoid disclosure; both grey.
- **Arkansas, FY2011 gift tax**: an IRS misprint, drawn as printed ($514,019 thousand; both identities close at $972,819 thousand).

## Our figures

- **Per resident**: with the Census Bureau's July 1 population estimate (FRED series <state>POP).
- **FY2025 dollars**: Real dollars: GDP price index (FRED GDPDEF, retrieved September 29, 2026), fiscal-year average, FY2025 dollars.
