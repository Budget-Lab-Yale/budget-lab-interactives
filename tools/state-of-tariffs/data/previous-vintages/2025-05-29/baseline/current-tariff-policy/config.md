**Current Tariff Policy**

Tariff actions in effect or announced as of May 29, 2025, shown alongside the retaliatory
actions announced in response. On May 28, 2025, the Court of International Trade ruled the
IEEPA-based tariffs (the China/Canada/Mexico fentanyl-trafficking tariffs and the April 2
worldwide reciprocal tariffs) unlawful; only the Section 232 tariffs on automobiles and steel and
aluminum remain reflected here on the U.S. side, while retaliatory actions taken in response to
the now-enjoined tariffs are shown as originally announced.

_Notes: * TBL assumes that 40% of automobile content in imported motor vehicles from Canada and
Mexico are of US origin. For the purposes of the auto tariff rebate, TBL assumes that 1/3 of
imported autos are assembled in the US. ** TBL simulated this as a prohibitive Chinese export
tariff on these commodities._
