Fiscal Impact
The current tariff regime, assuming that Section 122 tariffs expire, would raise about $1.3 trillion over ten years, conventionally scored. Given the negative output effects of the tariffs, these new revenues will be partially offset by reductions in tax revenue as a result of lower growth. TBL estimates that these effects would total about $10 billion over the decade, bringing net dynamic revenue to about $1.2 trillion.

If instead the Section 122 tariffs are extended, revenue would be meaningfully higher: conventional revenue would be $1.8 trillion over the decade and the dynamic score would be roughly $1.7 trillion.
