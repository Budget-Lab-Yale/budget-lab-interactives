## Overview

This is placeholder content for the **State of Tariffs** interactive. The structure, navigation, and chart-engine wiring are in place; the figures and text below are samples to be replaced with real content.

Use the tabs above to move between sections. Within each tab, the list on the left selects which figure to view; toggles and selectors (where present) switch between views of the same data.

## What this tool will cover

- **Tariff Levels** — the overall U.S. tariff rate over time, rates by trading partner, and a detailed rate table.
- **Revenue & Prices** — estimated tariff revenue and the pass-through of tariffs to consumer prices.

All numbers currently shown are illustrative placeholders, not Budget Lab estimates.
