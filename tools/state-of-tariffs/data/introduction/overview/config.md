## The State of Tariffs

This tool consolidates the Budget Lab's up-to-date analysis of U.S. tariffs. It has four parts:

- **Daily Statutory Rates** — the statutory tariff rate over time, overall and broken out by
  legal authority, trading partner, and product.
- **Default Scenario** — the projected economic and fiscal effects of the default tariff
  policy scenario.
- **Alternative Scenarios** — the same effects compared across all modeled policy scenarios.
- **Methodology** — how these estimates are produced.
