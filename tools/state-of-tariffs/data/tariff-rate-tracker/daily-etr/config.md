The ETR started the year at under 3 percent and reached over 22 percent after Liberation Day one year ago, before falling again, driven by a roller coaster of announced policy changes, trade deals, and legal challenges to the 2025 tariff regime. As each of these changes is incorporated into the HTS (or other similar legal documents), we update the tracker accordingly, including the projection of future rates according to current law.
