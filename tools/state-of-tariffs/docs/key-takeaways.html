Key Takeaways

  • As of July 20th, the average statutory tariff rate stands at 12%. Under current law, which includes a scheduled expiration of Section 122 tariffs, this figure is set to be 9.4% at the end of this year. If instead the administration imposes announced-but-not-implemented tariffs under Section 301, the rate will be 12.5% at the end of this year.
  • We estimate the ultimate consumer price impact of current-law tariff policy to be about 0.3%. If the Section 301 tariffs are imposed, that figure is more than twice as large.
  • Estimated household costs due to tariffs total about $500 annually under current law, and $1,100 if the Section 301 tariffs are imposed.
  • Over the next ten years, we estimate that current-law tariffs will raise about $1.2 trillion (or about $1.9 trillion under the Section 301 scenario). These numbers are somewhat lower, though, after taking into account the negative impact of tariffs on GDP.
