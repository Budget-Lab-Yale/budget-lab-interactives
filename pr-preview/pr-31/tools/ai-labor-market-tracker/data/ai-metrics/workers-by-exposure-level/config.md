Using the Eloundou et al. (2023) "GPT-Beta" exposure metric, the figure shows the shares of all workers who are in each of three groups: the lowest (bottom 40%), middle (next 40%), and top (top 20%) by AI-exposure rating. Shares do not sum to 100% because some workers are in occupations without exposure ratings.
